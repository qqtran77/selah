Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 • Published by 77AM LLC
Core Privacy Guarantee: Selah: Holy Bible is architected as an offline-first, privacy-respecting devotion application. We believe your prayers, confessions, and spiritual reflections are profoundly personal. We do not sell, track, upload, or collect your personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Selah does not require you to create an account, register an email address, or provide any identifying personal information to use any of its features. Specifically:

  • Zero Cloud Databases: We do not operate remote databases storing your private notes, prayer petitions, or confessions.
  • Zero Behavioral Tracking: We do not embed Facebook SDK, Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or third-party behavioral profiling trackers.
  • Zero Advertisements: We do not sell ad space or share advertising identifiers (IDFA) with data brokers.

2. On-Device Encryption & Security Architecture

All sensitive personal devotion data recorded within the application is protected using industry-grade cryptography:

  • AES-256-GCM Encryption: Your prayer intentions, answers to prayers, voice memos, and examination of conscience notes are encrypted using AES-256 in Galois/Counter Mode (GCM) with Authenticated Additional Data (AAD).
  • Hardware-Backed Keychain Storage: Cryptographic keys are generated locally and stored inside the iOS Keychain using kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly, preventing keys from ever leaving the physical device or migrating via iTunes/iCloud unencrypted backups.
  • Atomic Cryptographic Shredder: When you tap "Borrar ahora" (Shred Now) in the Confessional Companion or delete an intention, data is overwritten and purged immediately from local memory and storage.

3. Device Permissions & Microphone Usage

Selah requests device microphone access solely for the optional voice recording feature within Virtual Candle & Intentions:

  • Microphone Usage Description: The microphone is utilized strictly when you explicitly tap the record button to capture a personal spoken prayer petition.
  • Local Audio Encoding: Recorded audio is converted directly into on-device encrypted storage. At no point is your voice memo uploaded to any external server or transcribed through cloud speech APIs.

4. In-App Purchases & Apple Media Services

All payment transactions, subscription billing, and renewals are handled exclusively by Apple via your Apple ID account and official StoreKit 2 APIs. 77AM LLC never receives or stores your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details. Apple handles payment processing according to the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Selah is safe for users of all ages, including families and children. Because we collect zero personally identifiable information (PII) from any user, we comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and EU GDPR provisions.
