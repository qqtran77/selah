Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Selah completely offline?

Yes. All canonical scripture books, study reflections, reading plans, and personal notes operate 100% locally with zero internet connection required.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Selah on your device → tap More → tap Restore Purchases (or tap Restore on the onboarding screen). Your Apple ID receipt will be verified and access unlocked immediately.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

All subscription billing is managed by Apple. To cancel: iPhone Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Selah → Cancel Subscription. For refunds, visit Apple's portal at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How does the Confessional Shredder work?

Inside the Guía para el Confesionario, tapping "Borrar ahora" (Shred Now) immediately overwrites and unlinks all session notes from your device storage, leaving zero disk remnants.
